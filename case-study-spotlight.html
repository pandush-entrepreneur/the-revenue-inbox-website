Spotlight · PR software

Qualified opportunities across aviation, hotels and luxury resorts.

The context

Spotlight needed to reach teams that already receive a high volume of PR pitches. Buyers were not necessarily searching for a brand visibility and ranking platform, so the outreach had to make the product useful before asking for their time.

The offer

We offered a free article about the prospect's brand. A reply triggered an agent to draft a publish-ready piece, which was delivered before a sales conversation. No call was required to receive it.

The article served as a direct demonstration of the platform. Prospects could evaluate a finished asset before speaking to sales.

How the campaign ran

  • Separate campaigns for aviation, hotels and luxury resorts, with an article angle for each audience.
  • Sending volume increased from roughly 2,000 to more than 7,000 emails per day as deliverability held.
  • Replies were answered within five minutes, with agent-drafted responses approved by a human.
  • Follow-up continued for up to eight touchpoints, alongside re-engagement of earlier non-responders.

The results

The campaign sent 221,400 emails and generated 159 qualified opportunities in under 90 days. The recorded reply rate was 1.75%, with approximately 3,870 total replies.

The source tracks $159,000 in pipeline value. That is pipeline, rather than a claim about closed revenue. The free article remained part of the client's sales approach after the campaign.

Result snapshot

  • Marketing, communications and brand leaders.
  • Aviation, hotels and luxury resorts.
  • 159 qualified opportunities in under 90 days.
  • Approximately 53 opportunities per month.
  • One opportunity per 1,392 emails.
