The Inbox-to-Meeting System.

One team owns the path from your ideal buyer to your calendar. Strategy, data, copy, infrastructure, email, LinkedIn and booking stay in the same hands.

  1. Start with your buyer.

    We define your ideal customer profile (ICP) and build the data around it. A decision-maker who can buy your offer is the starting point.

  2. Give them a reason to reply.

    We write the outreach around your offer. The recruitment campaign produced 1 positive reply per 115 emails.

  3. Build the sending setup.

    We own the domains and mailboxes used for outreach. The $1,500/month infrastructure fee covers domains, mailboxes and databases.

  4. Own the conversation.

    We run email and LinkedIn outreach and handle replies. An interested reply still needs to become an agreed conversation.

  5. Put the meeting on your calendar.

    A qualified meeting is a booked call with a decision-maker inside your ICP who has agreed to hear your offer. You show up and close.
