What does The Revenue Inbox do?

We are a B2B appointment setting agency for SaaS and service companies. We own strategy, ideal customer profile, data, copy, sending infrastructure, email and LinkedIn outreach, reply handling and meeting booking. You show up and close.

What counts as qualified?

A booked call with a decision-maker who matches your ideal customer profile and has agreed to hear the offer. A positive reply by itself is not a qualified meeting.

I've been burned by an agency before.

Judge us by the work, the definitions and the terms. The case studies show the sending volume, replies and outcomes. The current promise is: 20 qualified meetings. 30 days. Or we work for free. The clock starts when campaigns go live.

How much does it cost?

$100 per qualified booked appointment, plus $1,500 per month for domains, mailboxes, data and sending tools. At 20 booked appointments in a month: $2,000 + $1,500 = $3,500. Rolling monthly. No long contract.

What happens if you miss the guarantee?

20 qualified meetings. 30 days. Or we work for free. Meetings are counted from the day campaigns go live, and they must meet the agreed ICP and qualification definition.

Will you use my main domain?

We set up and manage dedicated sending infrastructure. We will walk through the domains, mailboxes and sending setup on the strategy call. No agency can promise zero deliverability risk.

How much of this do I have to manage?

You explain the offer and take the booked calls. We own the strategy and day-to-day outbound work. You do not need to build lists, write outreach or manage the sending infrastructure.

Can this work in a small market?

Parking Compliance Systems reaches parking lot owners with a list built from physical locations. That campaign produces 26 qualified leads a day. Small markets need careful targeting and follow-up, not the same list everyone else buys.

Are you promising revenue?

No. We promise qualified meetings. Your close rate, contract value and sales cycle determine revenue. The calculator assumes an 80% show rate and shows its costs and conversion assumptions.
