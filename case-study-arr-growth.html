The context

Before working with The Revenue Inbox, the company had inconsistent lead flow from traditional channels, unpredictable meeting volume, and too many scattered outreach efforts. Forecasting revenue was difficult because lead generation was volatile.

What we did

  • Defined the ICP and wrote copy around a clear, relevant offer.
  • Set up sending infrastructure and managed deliverability.
  • Concentrated resources on cold email instead of dividing them across several channels.

Replies and follow-up

Every interested lead was answered within 5 minutes. A structured 8-touch cadence recovered prospects who did not engage at first. Retargeting campaigns reactivated older lead databases and created second and third chances with prospects who were not ready initially.

The results

The campaign produced 240 qualified meetings per month, almost zero no-shows, 20 interested responses in a few days, and a company record of 34 meetings booked in a single day from cold email alone.

The appointment volume led the sales team to run group sales meetings. The recorded growth came from cold email.

Result snapshot

  • 240 meetings booked every month.

  • 3 clients generated in one week.
