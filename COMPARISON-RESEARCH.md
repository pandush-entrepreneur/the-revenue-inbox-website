# Agency comparisons — research and publishing plan

Research checked 22 October 2026. Sources and reusable competitor statements live in [the landscape](content/competitors/landscape.json) and [Belkins facts](content/competitors/belkins.json).

## What this research can establish

These are relevant agencies competing for overlapping outbound budgets. This is a practical shortlist, not an exhaustive market census or a ranking by revenue, traffic or market share. Discovery searches included “Belkins alternatives” and “best B2B appointment setting cold email agencies.” Agency-authored roundups are discovery signals only; the factual summaries use official pages. General web results do not establish US Google rankings or keyword volumes.

Private GA4 and Search Console reporting were not connected during this work. Installed GA4 tracking does not give an agent reporting access. Windsor.ai was already suggested in this workspace and remains unconnected. GA4 can show how people reached our website and what they did here; it does not reveal the audiences visiting competitors. Search Console can identify relevant queries only when our own property has impressions for them. CRM and sales notes are needed to establish actual buyer overlap.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Belkins** — first full page; user-named, direct appointment-setting overlap. One canonical `/compare/belkins` page directly compares The Revenue Inbox with Belkins. The founder requested one competitor per page, with no other vendor recommendations.
2. **SalesHive, Martal Group, CIENCE** — next candidates, researched and written separately. Each compares a different scope: SDR coverage, broader sales outsourcing, or managed GTM.
3. **Cleverly, SalesRoads, Sopro, SalesBread, Sales.co** — strong second wave. Closely related channels or buying decisions, with important differences in leads versus booked meetings.
4. **Leadium, Callbox** — useful expansion candidates after demand and sales feedback validate priority.

LeadBird remains a verification watch item: the official page was not readable in this pass. Do not turn missing information into a negative claim.

Intent phrases are hypotheses, not measured demand. Before expanding the set, use connected GA4/GSC, location-specific keyword data and actual prospect mentions to adjust the order. Do not create thin copies with only competitor names changed. Software vendors belong in a separate agency-versus-DIY decision, not in this direct-agency list.

## Initial deliverable

- `/compare`: a concise directory linking to the completed Belkins comparison. Add further comparison links only when those pages are complete; no dead cards or empty pages.
- `/compare/belkins`: The Revenue Inbox vs. Belkins ? our advantages, a sourced side-by-side comparison, clear pricing examples, transition checklist, real case evidence, visible sources and matched FAQ schema.
- Shared “More” navigation and footer point to “How we compare.” Social cards use the current site identity.
- No production publication in this task until requested. Existing approved blog articles retain their approval state.

## Editorial and visual brief

Read mode, within the approved dark purple Manrope/DM Sans world. A founder comparing quotes needs service scope, billable outcomes, total cost and proof before a call. Use a readable decision guide, short anchored contents, a semantic table that scrolls locally on mobile, and editorial lists rather than a wall of feature cards. The first viewport states the commercial distinction. Eight concrete anchors: $100 meeting fee, Basic vendor ownership, Standard managed setup, Express's conditional day-3 start, the qualified-booking definition, the plan-specific guarantee, GRAIL's 12 meetings in 21 days, Networked's 81 meetings in a month. These verified terms become the page's decision framework. No decorative stock imagery or fabricated dashboards.

The incumbent reading-page composition is authoritative; this is an extension of the existing website. A native contents jump and FAQ expansion provide useful interaction with a complete HTML fallback. Competitor facts are attributed, dated and centrally stored. No scoring stars, unverified reviews, invented switchers or unsupported savings percentages.

## Comparison review workflow

The founder requested one new comparison at a time, with explicit approval before the next. Belkins, SalesHive, Martal Group, CIENCE and Cleverly are complete. The latest instruction authorizes final spacing/native-design polish and publication of the entire completed set. Further competitor pages still require the next approval.

SalesHive uses current official pricing and email-service sources. The comparison focuses on qualified-booking billing versus a flat-fee SDR program, the explicitly different meeting commitments and conditional launch timing. Do not infer exact savings from an unpublished SalesHive quote. The hub remains a concise directory of completed pages.

The pricing introduction and cost table now share a 760px maximum measure, with a 32px gap (24px on mobile) before the table or its scroll cue. Keep these rules across comparison pages. QA measures these gaps and both table edges on each viewport.
