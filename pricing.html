Before you choose a plan.

Is Basic free?

Basic has a $0 monthly agency base fee. You pay $100 per qualified booked meeting, and you pay vendors directly for your domains, mailboxes and tools. You share access with us so we can run the campaign.

What does Standard include?

Standard is $1,500 per month plus $100 per qualified booked meeting. We buy and manage the domains, mailboxes, data and sending tools, and run the campaign through to qualified booking.

What makes Express different?

Express is $2,500 per month plus $100 per qualified booked meeting. It includes everything in Standard, with prewarmed domains and mailboxes so campaigns can go live on day 3.

When does the Express launch clock start?

Three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete. The day-3 launch promise is separate from the 30-day meeting guarantee, which starts when campaigns go live.

Which plans include the guarantee?

Standard and Express include: 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free. Basic does not include the 20-meeting guarantee.

What counts as a qualified booked meeting?

A booked call with a decision-maker within the agreed ideal customer profile who has agreed to hear the offer. A positive reply on its own is not a qualified booked meeting. We agree the qualification criteria before launch.

Does Express promise more meetings?

Express changes launch speed by using prewarmed infrastructure. The $100 meeting fee, qualification definition and 20-meeting, 30-day guarantee are the same as Standard.
