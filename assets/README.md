# Asset provenance

All imagery comes from this repository or its existing public Vimeo videos. No generated imagery, stock photo or invented client logo is used.

- `client-0.webp` through `client-4.webp`: original Networked, Tecaudex, Brysa, Red Buffer and Jaycon assets, cropped to content bounds and normalized to monochrome at render. Transparent Networked/Jaycon contours recolored to existing ink for light-canvas legibility.
- `pandush.webp`, `umair.webp`: original founder photos, matched 4:5 crops. Monochrome treatment in CSS.
- `vimeo-<id>.webp`: public thumbnail still from the existing Vimeo video of the same ID, higher-resolution i.vimeocdn.com derivative; consistent 4:3 crop. Fourth crop moves down to retain the face. OEmbed origin manifests remain in evidence/.
- `investors-campaign.webp`, `arr-growth-campaign.webp`, `recruitment-campaign.webp`: factual dashboard crops from original case1-page-2, case2-page-3 and case3-page-2 JPGs.
- `investors-evidence-0.webp`, `arr-growth-evidence-0.webp`, `arr-growth-evidence-1.webp`: calendar/chart extracts from case1-page-6, case2-page-5 and case2-page-7. Original evidence remains in case-study-assets/.
- `font-*.woff2`: DM Sans and Manrope downloaded through Google Fonts, converted to WOFF2 and subset to Latin/extended Latin. Font-display: swap. Licenses: dmsans-OFL.txt and manrope-OFL.txt.
- Original `The Revenue - 1.png` and `og-image.png` are referenced unchanged from root.

Original unused images/PDFs remain at their existing paths. Only derivatives referenced by the final pages ship in this assets directory.
