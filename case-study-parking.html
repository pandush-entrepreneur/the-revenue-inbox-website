Parking compliance software

Outreach to parking owners and operators in the US.

The context

The client sells parking compliance software to parking lot owners and parking management companies in the US. These buyers can be difficult to identify through standard B2B databases. LinkedIn coverage was thin, websites were often outdated and verified contact details were limited.

The campaign also needed to explain a category many owners were unfamiliar with. A software feature list was not enough to start a useful conversation.

What we did

  • Built an owner and operator database by scraping, cross-referencing and enriching multiple sources.
  • Managed one master prospect pool in structured cycles, rather than treating the market as an unlimited list.
  • Separated messaging for private lots, mixed-use sites, municipal facilities and third-party operators.
  • Led with liability exposure and lost revenue, instead of product features.

How appointments were booked

Replies were handled within five minutes. Follow-up continued for up to eight touchpoints, with ongoing re-engagement of earlier non-responders.

Dedicated sending infrastructure was monitored daily. In a finite market, losing deliverability means losing access to prospects that cannot simply be replaced.

The latest results

In the last four weeks, the campaign sent 58,700 emails, recorded a 7.35% reply rate and generated 148 opportunities.

The original case study recorded an earlier 30-day snapshot of 50 opportunities from 50,500 emails at a 2.5% reply rate. The dashboard above uses the latest figures, rather than combining the two periods.
