Read the FAQs

Questions before the call.

What does a cold email agency do?

A cold email agency runs outbound email to reach prospective buyers and book sales conversations. The Revenue Inbox owns strategy, ICP, data, copy, sending infrastructure, email and LinkedIn outreach, reply handling, and meeting booking. You take the sales calls and close the deals.

What makes The Revenue Inbox different?

We have booked 1,000+ meetings from cold email in the last two years. Replies are handled within five minutes. Pricing is $100 per qualified booked appointment plus monthly infrastructure. The promise is 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free.

Will you send cold email from our main domain?

No. We use dedicated sending domains and mailboxes, not your main domain. Those domains and mailboxes are part of the sending infrastructure we own for the campaign. The $1,500 monthly infrastructure fee covers domains, mailboxes, data, and sending tools.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A qualified meeting is with a decision-maker inside the agreed ICP who has agreed to hear the offer. A positive reply alone is not a qualified meeting. That distinction matters: the appointment fee is for a qualified booked appointment, not simply for someone responding to outreach.

How much does the service cost, and how long is the contract?

The service costs $100 per qualified booked appointment plus $1,500 per month for infrastructure. Infrastructure includes domains, mailboxes, data, and sending tools. The agreement is rolling monthly, with no long contract.

How does the 20-meeting promise work?

The promise is 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free. The clock starts at campaign launch. A qualified meeting requires a decision-maker inside the agreed ICP who has agreed to hear the offer. Positive replies alone do not count toward the promise.

Is LinkedIn outreach included?

Yes. LinkedIn outreach is included alongside cold email. We own the copy and outreach, as well as strategy, ICP, data, sending infrastructure, reply handling, and meeting booking. You do not take over at the first positive reply. You take the sales calls and own the close.

Can cold email work in a small market?

A small market needs a closer look, not a blanket yes. Parking Compliance Systems reaches parking lot owners with a list built from physical locations, and that campaign produces 26 qualified leads a day. Small markets need careful targeting and follow-up, not the same list everyone else buys.
