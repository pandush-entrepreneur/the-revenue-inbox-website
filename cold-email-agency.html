Process

How the work runs.

Six stages. One owner from the first list to the meeting on your calendar.

  1. 01

    Define the buyer

    We agree the ICP, the offer angle and what counts as a qualified meeting before any list is built.

  2. 02

    Build the list

    Contacts are researched and checked against the ICP, with the exclusions agreed up front.

  3. 03

    Write the sequence

    First touch, follow-ups and reply angles written for that buyer, not a generic template.

  4. 04

    Set up sending

    Dedicated domains, mailboxes and warm-up managed on your behalf, separate from your main domain.

  5. 05

    Run the conversations

    Email and LinkedIn outreach, replies within five minutes, and follow-up until the lead books or says no.

  6. 06

    Book the meeting

    A decision-maker within the agreed ICP, on your calendar, with the context you need for the call.

After the reply

The reply is where most agencies stop.

A positive reply is not a meeting. The work between the two is where this service earns its fee.

  1. 01

    A human replies

    Within five minutes, a person answers the question, handles the objection and checks the fit.

  2. 02

    We qualify the meeting

    Decision-maker, agreed ICP, and a clear yes to hearing the offer. If it does not meet the definition, it does not count.

  3. 03

    We book the call

    We propose specific times and confirm the booking with the lead.

  4. 04

    You get the context

    Before the call, you know who the buyer is, what they asked and why they agreed to talk.

Questions about the service.

What is included in the service?

Strategy, ICP, prospect data, copy, email and LinkedIn outreach, reply handling and qualified meeting booking. Every plan charges $100 per qualified booked meeting. Basic has no monthly base fee; you buy vendor services and share access. Standard is $1,500/month with managed infrastructure. Express is $2,500/month with prewarmed domains and mailboxes and a day-3 launch.

What do you need from us before launch?

Your offer, the buyer you want to reach, one person who can approve the ICP and copy, and sales capacity to take the calls. On Basic, we also need access to your sending setup. Express goes live on day 3: three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete.

Do you write the copy, or do we?

We write the first touch, follow-ups and reply angles. You approve the voice and any claims about the product. The copy has to sound like you and survive a real buyer reading it, so your review is part of the build.

Will you send from our main domain?

We use dedicated sending domains and mailboxes. On Standard and Express, we buy and manage them. On Basic, you pay vendors directly and share access with us. We do not use your main domain for campaign sending.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A booked call with a decision-maker within the agreed ICP who has agreed to hear the offer. A positive reply is not a qualified meeting. That definition is agreed before the campaign starts and is the one used for the appointment fee and the 30-day promise.

What happens when someone replies?

A human answers within five minutes. We handle the question or objection, qualify the fit and propose specific times. If the lead is ready to talk, we make the booking easy. If not, we follow up until there is a clear yes or no.

Can cold email work in a small market?

A small market needs a closer look, not a blanket yes. Parking Compliance Systems reaches parking lot owners with a list built from physical locations, and that campaign produces 26 qualified leads a day. The question is whether the ICP contains enough decision-makers for your offer.

What does the 30-day promise cover?

On Standard and Express: 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free. Meetings must meet the agreed ICP and qualification definition. Basic does not include the 20-meeting guarantee. Positive replies alone do not count.
