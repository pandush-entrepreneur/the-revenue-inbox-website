Why choose The Revenue Inbox?

Know your costs before the first call.

Every plan has the same $100 qualified-meeting fee. Choose Basic at $0 per month in agency base fees, Standard at $1,500 per month or Express at $2,500 per month. Basic vendor bills are extra; Standard and Express include managed infrastructure.

Keep your focus on the sales conversation.

We handle the ICP, research, copy, email and LinkedIn outreach, replies and booking. You meet the prospects and close. A billable meeting means a booked call with a decision-maker inside your agreed ICP who has agreed to hear the offer.

Put a clear commitment behind the campaign.

Standard and Express include 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free. Basic does not include that guarantee. The meeting definition and acceptance criteria are agreed before launch.

Launch on day 3 with Express.

Express uses prewarmed domains and mailboxes. Campaigns go live three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete. It gives teams ready to start a defined launch timeline.

Our service focuses on email-led appointment setting. Belkins offers additional channels in its upper package, including paid advertising and account-based marketing, and a partner-delivered small-business option. Compare the scope in your quote if those services are part of your brief. Belkins plans and pricing.

If you are changing agencies.

Before moving a live campaign, check your existing agreement and confirm what you can transfer. We would review the following with you before choosing a launch plan:

  • Access and ownership for domains, mailboxes, sending tools and CRM records.
  • Suppression lists, opt-outs and existing conversations, so outreach does not restart blindly.
  • Lead lists, approved messaging and the qualification rules your sales team uses.
  • Sending history and domain health. Existing infrastructure needs review before reuse.
  • Responsibility for replies and booked meetings during the handover.

A handover does not automatically qualify for Express. Its three-day clock starts only once payment, required inputs and approvals are complete.

Questions before you decide.

Is The Revenue Inbox a Belkins alternative?

Yes, for B2B teams looking for managed email and LinkedIn outreach through to qualified booked meetings. Choose based on the channels, service scope and billing model your team needs.

Is The Revenue Inbox cheaper than Belkins?

We publish our plan fees, but we cannot establish a like-for-like saving without your Belkins quote. Compare the same channels, infrastructure, qualification rules and contract period. Basic vendor bills are additional to our meeting fees.

Does Basic include the 20-meeting guarantee?

No. The guarantee of 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free, applies to Standard and Express only.

How quickly can an Express campaign launch?

Three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete. Express uses prewarmed domains and mailboxes. The launch promise is separate from the 30-day meeting guarantee.
