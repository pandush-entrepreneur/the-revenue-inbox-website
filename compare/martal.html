Why choose The Revenue Inbox?

Keep your closers on the calls.

If your founder or sales team already handles discovery, demos and negotiations, our service fills the earlier part of the process. We define the audience, research prospects, write the outreach, run email and LinkedIn campaigns, handle replies and book qualified calls.

Your team keeps the sales conversation and customer relationship. You can assess our work against the quality of the opportunities reaching your calendar.

Agree what you are paying for.

A qualified booked meeting is a call with a decision-maker inside your agreed ICP who has agreed to hear the offer. A positive reply alone does not count. We charge $100 per qualified booked meeting, on top of your chosen monthly base fee.

Basic keeps the agency base fee at $0 while you buy the domains, mailboxes and tools directly. Standard and Express let us buy and manage the infrastructure. The choice is visible before you book a call.

Put a meeting commitment behind the campaign.

Standard and Express include 20 qualified meetings in 30 days from campaign launch, or we work for free. Basic does not include that guarantee. Agree the qualification criteria and confirm the remedy in your agreement.

Have a defined launch point with Express.

Express campaigns go live on day 3: three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete. We use prewarmed domains and mailboxes. The launch promise is separate from the 30-day meeting guarantee.

Martal advertises a two-week launch on its cold email page. Confirm the starting conditions of its proposal before comparing timelines. Martal Group cold email service.

Choose the scope your team actually needs.

For teams that want qualified booking while keeping sales in-house, our plans focus on that handoff. If you also need discovery calls, closing support or ongoing account management outsourced, Martal’s higher tiers address a broader brief. Martal Group pricing and service tiers.

If you are changing agencies.

Before moving a live campaign, check your existing agreement and confirm what you can transfer. We would review the following with you before choosing a launch plan:

  • Access and ownership for domains, mailboxes, sending tools and CRM records.
  • Suppression lists, opt-outs and existing conversations, so outreach does not restart blindly.
  • Lead lists, approved messaging and the qualification rules your sales team uses.
  • Sending history and domain health. Existing infrastructure needs review before reuse.
  • Responsibility for replies and booked meetings during the handover.

A handover does not automatically qualify for Express. Its three-day clock starts only once payment, required inputs and approvals are complete.

Before you choose the scope.

Does Martal Group also offer appointment setting?

Yes. Its Tier 1A outbound service includes appointment booking. Compare that scope with our qualified-booking service before considering its broader sales and account-management tiers.

What would our own sales team still handle?

Your team runs the booked sales calls, discovery, demos, negotiation and closing. The Revenue Inbox handles the outbound campaign through to qualified booking; we do not provide a closing or customer account-management service.

Is the Basic plan free?

Basic has a $0 monthly agency base fee. You pay $100 per qualified booked meeting and buy your domains, mailboxes and tools directly from vendors. Their bills are additional. Basic does not include the 20-meeting guarantee.

When does the Express launch clock begin?

Three calendar days after payment, required inputs and approvals are complete. Express uses prewarmed domains and mailboxes. The 30-day meeting-guarantee clock begins when campaigns go live.
